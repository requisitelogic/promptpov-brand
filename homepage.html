How it works

Three steps. No admin rights.

1

Download & activate

Download the macOS app and activate the camera extension. One-click activation through System Settings. No sudo, no admin rights — works on managed Macs.

2

Pick the virtual camera

Choose "promptPOV Virtual Camera" in your meeting app. Works with Zoom, Microsoft Teams, FaceTime, Google Meet, Webex, QuickTime, and any other app that lets you choose a camera.

3

Configure widgets

Drag, snap, resize in the dashboard. Changes sync to your Mac in seconds. Your AI keys stay in your local Keychain — promptPOV never sees them.
