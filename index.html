07 — Decision Summary
promptpov
Best if you want to position as a tool-first product in the linear/notion tradition. Works when your audience is dev-adjacent or design-forward. Loses meaning of POV.
promptPOV ← recommended
Best overall. iPhone/FaceTime convention — everyone understands it. Signals the acronym. Lowercase prompt feels human + accessible; uppercase POV carries weight. Works at every size, in every context.
PromptPOV
Best for mainstream + non-technical audiences. YouTube-style. Clear, readable, two-word compound. Feels slightly more "product" than "brand." Good fallback if promptPOV tests poorly in surveys.
